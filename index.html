Runs on hardwareDec 2025 – Apr 2026

The right arm passes a red bowl to the left arm, which then picks up a box and places it in a green bowl. The left arm can only act once the right arm has finished, so one policy has to coordinate both arms over time.

A coding agent as a driving policy

In progressOct 2026 – present

Instead of a pre-trained VLA, a coding agent acts as a zero-shot visuomotor controller, following the code-as-policy approach of NVIDIA's CAPX (2026).

  • A lightweight 3D simulator exposes top-down, chase and front camera APIs. The agent calibrates the physics and sends motor commands (vx, vy, ω) through GET and POST calls only; it never sees the simulator's code.
  • Evaluating it under randomized goals and moving obstacles, with a CARLA version planned.
  • SmolVLA
  • ROS2
  • CARLA
  • Claude Code

World models for manipulation, built from scratch

In progressNov 2026 – present

Implementing world models from first principles along the simulator–renderer–planner taxonomy, starting with Mini-Dreamer (a continuous latent simulator trained with reconstruction) and Mini-IRIS (a token-space agent for video games).

  • Comparing latent-space prediction (JEPA-style, no pixel reconstruction) with pixel-space models such as IRIS on representation, training signal and inference latency.
  • Goal: a learned world model on the SO-101 arm that plans in imagination instead of real-world rollouts, alongside the HIL-SERL thesis.
